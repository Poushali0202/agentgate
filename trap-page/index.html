Product Content Security Test

These pages simulate product websites read by an AI shopping agent. The safe page contains ordinary product information. The malicious page contains instructions that attempt to manipulate the shopping agent.

SAFE PRODUCT PAGE

Normal product page

Contains product metadata, price, inventory, features, and reviews. No suspicious instructions are present.

MALICIOUS PRODUCT PAGE

Prompt-injection test page

Contains product data plus instructions attempting to override the customer's shopping request.

The Apify e-commerce scraper extracts product data. AgentGate analyzes the page content and decides whether the shopping request is safe.
